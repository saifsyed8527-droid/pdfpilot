These are third-party estimates, not Google traffic or indexing measurements. The underlying current backlink export and GSC performance tables were not available for verification. We therefore cannot identify valuable URLs to remove, assert link quality, or attribute the traffic estimate to a penalty.

No outreach was sent. The closed Chris Wheeler resource page is excluded.

Government and education materials already demonstrate demand for practical instructions around PDF utilities: the [Goiás health department manual](https://goias.gov.br/saude/wp-content/uploads/sites/34/2017/04/manual-de-uso-do-site-ilovepdf-2019-07-11-1-7f3.pdf) and [IFG digital-study material](https://www.ifg.edu.br/attachments/article/19169/Inform%C3%A1tica%20b%C3%A1sica%20para%20o%20estudo%20on-line%20%2819-12-2020%29.pdf) are examples of reference formats, not confirmed outreach prospects or promised placements. Check currency, ownership and editorial fit before contacting anyone.

Success measures: relevant editorial references, referral sessions that start/complete a PDF task, useful product feedback and new non-branded search impressions. Keep new referring domains separate from conversions. Do not buy links, manufacture guest-post networks, exchange irrelevant links, or pursue mass directory submissions. A useful asset and editorial judgment are prerequisites; links are not guaranteed.

Before any consolidation or removal, obtain URL-level referring links, GSC queries/clicks and current redirect history. Preserve useful destinations and use a one-hop redirect only for a genuine intent-equivalent replacement.
