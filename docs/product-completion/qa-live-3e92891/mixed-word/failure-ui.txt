How to use PDF to Word

Extract selectable text or use English OCR to produce editable Word paragraphs.

Input: application/pdf. Output: application/vnd.openxmlformats-officedocument.wordprocessingml.document.

Select a PDF.
Choose the available text extraction or OCR mode.
Convert and download Word, then proofread the output.

How are my files processed?
